# Databento's terms

A summary, not legal advice. Read Databento's own terms and each dataset's
licensing on databento.com before relying on it.

- **Plans.** Historical data on every plan, usage-based (billed by volume)
  or flat; live data from the Standard plan up. Databento passes each
  venue's licence fees through to you.
- **Live licences** are per dataset, set up through Databento's licensing
  questionnaire: your subscriber status (personal or commercial) and use
  (display or non-display) decide the venue's fees. Databento US Equities
  Mini carries no venue fee, for personal and commercial use; OPRA's
  personal licence is per device, and a firm's use is under OPRA's own
  agreement.
- **Distribution.** Showing data to outside parties, or redistributing it,
  is a separate entitlement and depends on each venue's terms.

## What the plugin declares

So the plugin declares each dataset's default terms conservatively: shown to
people, derived data for internal use only, never redistributed; OPRA's one
person's. Your deployment's Data sources page records the licence you enter
and warns when more than one person can read a plugin entitled to a
one-person dataset. Open Meridian does not decide whether your use meets
Databento's or a venue's terms; the licence records what you entered.

endorsed by Databento, Inc.

## Agents

An agent connected to your deployment may pass what it reads to its model's
host; whether that is allowed is for Databento's and the venue's terms to
say.
